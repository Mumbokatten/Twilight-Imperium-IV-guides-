Alliance Commander Ranking (1-31)

Ranking Criteria (General)

  • Universal value (works for most factions)
  • Economy/resource generation
  • Combat improvements
  • Flexibility and utility
  • Ease of unlock

Complete Ranking

1. Nomad (Navarch Feng) - Unlock: Have 1 scored secret objective - Ability: Produce flagship without spending resources - Why: Saves 8 resources. Always good value.

2. Crimson Rebellion (Ahk Siever) - Unlock: Place breach token in system with another player’s unit - Ability: At end of combat, gain commodity/TG - Why: Combat-focused faction. Addresses TG access weakness.

3. Deepwrought (Aello) - Unlock: Have an ocean card in play - Ability: When others research tech, gain commodity/TG if they take -1 discount - Why: Passive TG income. Addresses TG access weakness.

4. Muaat (Magmus) - Unlock: Produce a war sun - Ability: After you spend strategy token, gain 1 TG - Why: Passive TG income. Addresses TG access weakness.

5. Winnu (Rickar Rickani) - Unlock: Control MR or enter combat in MR system - Ability: Apply +2 combat in MR, home system, legendary planet systems - Why: Strong combat boost in key battles.

6. Titans of Ul (Tungstantus) - Unlock: Have 5 structures on game board - Ability: Gain 1 TG when using PRODUCTION - Why: Passive TG income every production.

7. Empyrean (Xuange) - Unlock: Be neighbors with all other players - Ability: After another player moves ships into system with your token, return token to reinforcements - Why: Late game slaying tool.

8. Vuil’raith Cabal (That Which Molds Flesh) - Unlock: Have units in 3 gravity rifts - Ability: Up to 2 fighters/infantry don’t count against PRODUCTION limit - Why: Extra production capacity. Not free - still cost resources but excluded from production limit.

9. Nekro Virus (Nekro Acidos) - Unlock: Own 3 technologies - Ability: After you gain tech, draw 1 action card - Why: Tech-heavy factions benefit.

10. Barony of Letnev (Rear Admiral Farran) - Unlock: Have 5 non-fighter ships in 1 system - Ability: After unit uses Sustain Damage, gain 1 TG - Why: TG from dreadnoughts with sustain damage.

11. Naaz-Rokha (Dart and Tai) - Unlock: Have mechs in 3 systems - Ability: After you gain control of planet from another player, explore that planet - Why: Extra value from conquests.

12. Mentak Coalition (S’ula Mentarion) - Unlock: Have 4 cruisers on game board - Ability: Force opponent to give PN after winning space combat - Why: Steal promissory notes after combat victories.

13. Federation of Sol (Claire Gibson) - Unlock: Control planets with 12+ resources - Ability: Place 1 infantry when defending ground combat - Why: Defensive infantry.

14. Jol-Nar (Ta Zern) - Unlock: Own 8 technologies - Ability: Reroll unit ability dice - Why: Reroll unit abilities (BOMBARDMENT, AFB, SPACE CANNON).

15. Argent Flight (Trrakan Aun Zulok) - Unlock: Have 6 units with AFB, SPACE CANNON, or BOMBARDMENT - Ability: Roll 1 additional die for unit abilities - Why: Extra die for unit abilities.

16. Yssaril Tribes (So Ata) - Unlock: Have 7 action cards - Ability: Look at action cards/PNs/secrets after activating system with your units - Why: Information gathering.

17. Ral Nel Consortium (Watchful OJZ) - Unlock: Be last player to pass during action phase - Ability: When you declare retreat, immediately retreat up to 2 ships to adjacent system without other players’ ships. Place command token from reinforcements into that system. - Why: Enhanced retreat mechanics. TE faction.

18. Ghosts of Creuss (Sai Seravus) - Unlock: Have units in 3 systems with alpha/beta wormholes - Ability: Place fighters after moving through wormholes - Why: Wormhole-dependent mobility.

19. Clan of Saar (Rowl Sarring) - Unlock: Have 3 space docks on game board - Ability: Place fighters/infantry at any space dock when producing - Why: Production flexibility across multiple docks.

20. Sardakk N’orr (G’hom Sek’kus) - Unlock: Control 5 planets in non-home systems - Ability: Commit 1 ground force from adjacent planets during invasion - Why: Invasion flexibility.

21. Obsidian (Aroz Hollow) - Unlock: Have units in The Fracture - Ability: Apply +1 combat in The Fracture - Why: Late game Styx plays. TE faction.

22. Yin Brotherhood (Brother Omar) - Unlock: Use one of your faction abilities - Ability: Green prereq + skip prereqs when researching others’ tech - Why: Tech path flexibility.

23. Last Bastion (Nip and Tuck) - Unlock: There are 3 galvanized units on game board - Ability: Action cards cannot be canceled by Sabotage. Nekro cannot place assimilator tokens on your components. - Why: Sabotage immunity and Nekro protection. TE faction.

24. Xxcha Kingdom (Elder Qanoj) - Unlock: Control planets with 12+ influence - Ability: Each planet exhausted to cast votes provides 1 additional vote - Why: +1 vote per planet. Strong for influence-heavy factions.

25. Council Keleres (Suffi An) - Unlock: Spend 1 TG after playing action card with component action - Ability: Perform additional action after component action - Why: Very situational.

26. Arborec (Dirzuga Rophal) - Unlock: Have 12 ground forces on planets you control - Ability: Produce 1 unit when another player activates system with your PRODUCTION units - Why: Reactive production ability.

27. L1Z1X Mindnet (2RAM) - Unlock: Have 4 dreadnoughts on game board - Ability: Ignore Planetary Shield for BOMBARDMENT - Why: Strong for BOMBARDMENT-heavy factions. Bypass defensive structures.

28. Hacan (Gila the Silvertongue) - Unlock: Have 10 trade goods - Ability: Spend TGs for 2 votes each - Why: Convert TG wealth into political power.

29. Naalu Collective (M’aban) - Unlock: Have ground forces in/adjacent to MR - Ability: Look at neighbors’ PNs and top/bottom agenda card - Why: Information gathering.

30. Firmament (Captain Aroz) - Unlock: Have a plot card in play - Ability: Treat planets in systems with ships as controlled for scoring secrets - Why: Fake control for secret objectives. TE faction.

31. Mahact Gene-Sorcerers (No Alliance Commander) - Note: Mahact has no alliance commander ability.

Notes

  • This is a general ranking that can be adjusted for specific faction synergies
  • When creating faction guides, use this as a starting point and move commanders up/down based on faction needs
  • Mahact excluded (no alliance commander ability)
  • Rankings will be adjusted based on user feedback
